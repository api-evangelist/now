DNOW is proud to announce the publication of its 2025 Corporate Sustainability Report, highlighting the company's progress in environmental responsibility, social impact and corporate governance last year, before and after its successful acquisition of MRC Global. What is included in DNOW's 2025 Corporate Sustainability Report? The report outlines how DNOW is creating long-term value through responsible business practices, operational efficiency, community engagement, supplier diversity initiatives and sustainable solutions that help customers reduce environmental impact.
